# Day 1 scenarios

- **Uniform:** all eight branches are 600 W at ideal equal flow. Expected outlet: 40 degC.
- **Nonuniform:** powers vary from 450 to 750 W while total heat stays at 4.8 kW. Expected outlets: 37.5–42.5 degC.
- **Restricted branch:** branch 4 receives half the ideal flow at 600 W. Its coolant rise doubles to 20 degC and its outlet reaches 50 degC.

These are prescribed screening cases. Day 1 does not yet solve pressure-based flow redistribution, pump behavior, or chip junction temperature.
